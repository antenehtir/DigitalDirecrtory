Ethiopia's Free Health Facility Finder

Antex MedDirectory is a completely free, community-powered health facility directory built for Addis Ababa. Whether you're a doctor, nurse, or patient, find the right facility — fast.

We list hospitals, specialty centers, diagnostic labs, telemedicine services, pharmacies, ambulance providers, home care services, medical plazas, and healthcare financing — all in one place. No sign-up. No fees. Forever free.
